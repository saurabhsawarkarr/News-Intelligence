Problem Statement
Project Title
AI-Powered Indian Stock Market News Intelligence Platform
Background
Indian stock market traders and researchers rely on multiple financial news platforms such as Moneycontrol, Economic Times, LiveMint, Business Standard, and Reuters India to stay informed about market-moving events.
However, important news is scattered across different sources, many articles report the same event, and traders spend significant time reading repetitive articles before understanding how the news may impact different sectors of the Indian stock market.
For active traders, speed is critical. Delays in identifying relevant market news can lead to missed trading opportunities or delayed decision-making.
There is a need for a centralized platform that continuously monitors trusted Indian financial news sources, removes duplicate stories, and uses Artificial Intelligence to summarize the news and explain its potential impact on the Indian stock market.
Objective
Design and build an AI-powered news intelligence platform that automatically collects financial news from multiple trusted Indian news sources, analyzes each news article using a Large Language Model (LLM), and presents concise, actionable insights for traders and market researchers.
The platform should help users understand:
What happened?
Which sector is likely to be affected?
Whether the news is positive or negative from an Indian stock market perspective.
Why that sector may be impacted.
The platform is not intended to predict stock prices or provide investment advice. Its purpose is to organize and explain market-relevant news in a structured and time-efficient manner.
News Sources
The application should aggregate news from multiple trusted Indian financial news sources.
Examples include:
Moneycontrol
Economic Times Markets
LiveMint
Business Standard
Reuters India (where available)
The system should focus only on news relevant to the Indian stock market.
The following categories should be excluded:
Cryptocurrency
Entertainment
Celebrity News
Sports
General lifestyle content
Any article that has no meaningful impact on listed Indian companies or market sectors
(Need to discuss this)
System Workflow
1. News Collection
The application should automatically fetch the latest financial news every one hour using multiple RSS feeds.
2. Duplicate Detection
Since multiple publishers often report the same event, the platform should identify duplicate stories and merge them into a single news item.
Instead of displaying five nearly identical articles, users should see one consolidated news item with multiple source references.
3. AI Analysis
Each unique news article should be analyzed using an LLM.
The model should generate:
Short summary (2–3 lines)
Market sentiment
Positive
Negative
Neutral
Primary affected sector
Secondary affected sector (if applicable)
Reasoning explaining why the sector may be affected
Important
Sentiment must be evaluated from the perspective of the Indian stock market, not from emotional sentiment.
Example:
RBI repo rate cut → Positive for Banking, Auto and Real Estate
Increase in crude oil prices → Negative for Aviation and Paint companies
The system should focus on explaining sector-level impact rather than predicting individual stock movements.
4. Data Storage
The platform should store:
Original news article metadata
AI-generated analysis
Source information
Timestamp
Historical data should be preserved to allow users to browse previous news.

5. Dashboard
The frontend should present news in a clean dashboard.
Each news card should display:
Headline
AI-generated summary
Positive / Negative badge
Primary affected sector
Secondary affected sector (if available)
AI reasoning
Published time
News source(s)
Original article link
Filters
Users should be able to filter news by:
Positive
Negative
Date
Historical news should remain accessible through archive support.
Refresh Frequency
The system should automatically refresh every one hour.

Non-Functional Requirements
The platform should:
Avoid duplicate news
Minimize unnecessary LLM calls
Store AI-generated analysis for future viewing
Support future addition of premium news APIs
Be modular and scalable

The first version will not include:
Stock price prediction
Buy/Sell recommendations
Company-level stock recommendations
Real-time alerts
Push notifications
Portfolio tracking

Cryptocurrency news

What important market news occurred recently.
