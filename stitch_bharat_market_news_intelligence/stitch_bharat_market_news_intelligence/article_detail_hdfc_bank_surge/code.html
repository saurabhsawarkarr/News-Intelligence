Shares of HDFC Bank saw significant upward momentum in early trading on Monday, driven by a robust set of quarterly numbers that largely surpassed analyst expectations. The private sector lender reported a healthy 33.5% year-on-year jump in its standalone net profit for the quarter ended December 2023, touching ₹16,372 crore.

The surge in profitability was underpinned by steady loan growth and an improvement in asset quality. Gross Non-Performing Assets (GNPA) improved to 1.26% from 1.34% a quarter ago, reflecting prudent risk management amidst a tightening liquidity environment. Net Interest Income (NII), a core metric representing the difference between interest earned and interest expended, grew by 23.9% to ₹28,471 crore.

Market analysts were particularly encouraged by the bank's ability to maintain its Net Interest Margin (NIM) at 3.4% on total assets, demonstrating pricing power and an effective liability strategy following its mega-merger with HDFC Ltd. Trading volumes spiked, with over 15 million shares changing hands within the first hour of market opening.
